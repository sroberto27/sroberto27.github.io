3 · Result

Sphere coverage is measured before gap-filling and weighted by cos(latitude), so it reflects solid angle rather than equirect pixels. Anything materially under 100% is a capture gap, not a stitching fault — no amount of alignment can fill a direction the lens never faced.

Blend modes, side by side

Rendered at full output resolution and scrolled, not fit to the card — the difference between these modes is a few pixels wide and vanishes under downscaling. Blended is the pre-Phase-1 behaviour: every overlapping source averaged, which draws a misaligned edge twice. Sharpest wins gives each output pixel to a single source outright. The judgement to make is whether doubled edges resolve to single instances without gaining visible seam lines.
